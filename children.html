Families & children

Children's privacy

Read this with the Privacy Policy. It is written for parents, guardians, and Google Play’s families rules.

Is this a kids’ app?

Snake Bite: Mind Puzzle is a general-audience puzzle. Families can play it. Version 1.0.0 is not enrolled in a Designed for Families programme unless the store listing later says so. Always follow the age rating shown on Google Play.

What children can and cannot do

  • Play puzzles offline, with no timer.
  • Pick a local nickname and avatar. That stays on the device. We do not ask for a real name, school, or home address.
  • There is no chat, no friends list, no public profile, and no user-generated feed.
  • There are no in-app purchases, so a child cannot spend money in the game.
  • The game shows Google AdMob ads: interstitial ads after some level clears, and rewarded video when someone taps Hint. Ads are not used to ask a child for a real name, chat, or an account.
  • Hints are optional. A player can finish levels without watching a rewarded ad.

Information from children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for our own servers. AdMob may process advertising identifiers and related data as described in the Privacy Policy and Google’s policies. If a parent believes a child has sent personal information to waseemdeveloper521@gmail.com, email us and we will delete that message from the inbox.

Parental controls

You can use Android Digital Wellbeing, Play parental controls, and the app’s own sound/haptics switches. To limit ads system-wide, use Google’s advertising settings and family controls on the device. To remove local progress, follow Delete your data.
