Privacy Policy

Effective 17 October 2026  ·  App: Snake Bite: Mind Puzzle  ·  Package: com.snakebitepuzzle.app

This policy explains what Snake Bite: Mind Puzzle (“the app”, “we”) does with information on your device. It is written for Google Play’s User Data policy and matches version 1.0.0 of the app that includes Google AdMob advertising.

1. Who we are

The app is published under package name com.snakebitepuzzle.app. For privacy questions, email waseemdeveloper521@gmail.com.

2. Short version

The game does not create an account and does not need your email, name, or phone number to play. Level progress and settings stay on your device. Puzzles can be played offline. The game shows ads through Google AdMob (interstitial ads between some levels, and rewarded video ads when you choose Hint). AdMob may send advertising data off the device to Google. Ads need a network connection. There are no in-app purchases in this version.

4. Information we do not collect

  • No account, login, or password.
  • No contacts, photos, precise GPS location permission, microphone recording, or camera.
  • No payment information. This version has no in-app purchases.
  • No health, financial, or government ID data.
  • We do not run our own game analytics or cloud save server.

5. Google AdMob

We use the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob publisher ID pub-5232244483655690) to show:

  • Interstitial ads — full-screen ads at natural breaks, such as after you clear a level and tap Next level (not every level; frequency is limited).
  • Rewarded video ads — optional full-screen ads you choose when you tap Hint. Watching to the end unlocks one hint step. Closing early does not grant a hint.

The SDK may collect and share data with Google for serving ads, measuring ads, analytics, fraud prevention, and related purposes. That can include:

  • Approximate location (often from IP address; the app does not request a GPS permission for this)
  • Device and advertising identifiers (for example Android Advertising ID)
  • App activity related to ads (for example ad impressions and clicks)
  • Diagnostics and crash information related to the ads SDK

Google’s own policies apply to that processing. Read Google Privacy Policy and How AdMob uses data.

We do not sell your level progress. Game saves are not uploaded to us. Ad data is handled by Google, not stored on a game server we run.

Authorized sellers for our app are listed in app-ads.txt on this website (for AdMob / app-ads.txt verification).

6. Consent (EEA, UK, and similar regions)

Where required by law, the app uses Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to ask for consent before personalized ads are requested. You can change ad personalization in your device’s Google advertising settings. That does not always stop every AdMob request (for example non-personalized or limited ads may still appear).

7. Permissions

The Android build may request MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS so in-game sound can play correctly. That permission is not used to record audio. Showing ads and loading ad creatives uses the device’s internet connection.

8. Sharing

We do not sell your puzzle progress. Local game data (levels, settings, nickname) is not sent to an advertiser by us.

AdMob shares advertising data with Google, which is a third party. Google Play may also process install and crash statistics when you download the app from the store.

9. Children

The game is a general-audience puzzle. It is not a social network and has no chat. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for our own use. AdMob may still process advertising data as described above. See also Children's privacy.

10. How long data is kept

Local game data stays on the device until you clear it. Uninstalling the app, or clearing the app’s storage in Android settings, deletes it. See Delete your data. Advertising data retained by Google is governed by Google’s policies.

11. Your choices

  • Turn sound, music, and haptics off in Settings.
  • Change nickname and avatar on the home screen.
  • Skip Hint if you do not want to watch a rewarded ad.
  • Adjust Google ad personalization in system settings; complete or dismiss the in-app consent form where it is shown.
  • Delete local data as described on the delete-data page.
  • Email us at waseemdeveloper521@gmail.com if something here does not match what you see in the app.

12. Security

Because progress is stored only on the device, anyone with access to the unlocked phone can see or change that local profile. We do not transmit that profile to our servers. Ad traffic uses Google’s SDK and network security.

13. International users

Gameplay data is designed to stay on the device. If you are in the EEA, UK, Switzerland, or a similar region, consent tools may appear before personalized ads. You can still play puzzles without creating an account.

14. Changes

If we add accounts, new ad formats, analytics, or cloud saves, we will update this policy and the date at the top before that version is published. Please check this page after app updates.
