User Data policy

Delete your data

Snake Bite: Mind Puzzle does not create a cloud account. There is nothing on our servers to delete.

What exists

The only player data we store is on your phone: level progress, settings, a local nickname, and an avatar choice. We cannot see that data from here.

Advertising identifiers and related ad data may be processed by Google AdMob on the device and by Google. Clearing app storage removes the game’s local save; it does not erase Google’s own advertising records. Use your device’s Google advertising settings if you want to reset or limit the advertising ID.

How to delete it

  1. On your Android device open Settings.
  2. Go to Apps (or Applications) and find Snake Bite.
  3. Tap Storage, then Clear storage or Clear data.
  4. Or uninstall the app. That also removes the local save.

After that, the next launch behaves like a new player.

Why there is no “delete my account” form

Google Play requires an in-app or web way to delete an account when an app lets you create one. This game does not sign you in and does not store a player profile on a server, so there is no account to close. Clearing app storage is the complete deletion method.
