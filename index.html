How to play

1. Drag

Slide a snake along empty tiles. It only goes where there is space.

2. Food

Guide it to its matching fruit. Cover every square on the way.

3. Solve

Exact fit wins the level. Watch the crates — one bump and that snake is gone.

What you get

Many levels

Easy at first. Gradually harder later.

No timers

Think at your own pace. There are no lives and no countdown.

Play offline

Puzzles and progress stay on your device. You can solve boards without the internet.

Light & dark

Switch theme whenever you like.

Optional hints

Stuck? Watch a short rewarded ad for one hint step. Or keep solving without it.

Free to play

No account and no in-app purchases. Supported by occasional interstitial ads and optional rewarded ads via Google AdMob.
