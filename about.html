Ads in the app

  • Interstitial — shown at natural breaks after some wins when the player taps Next level.
  • Rewarded — shown only when the player taps Hint; watching grants one hint move.
  • No banner ads on the puzzle board.
  • No in-app purchases in this version.

Data safety summary

Use this when you fill in Play Console Data safety. It must match the live app.

  • Game progress is not collected on our servers.
  • Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) collects and shares advertising data. Declare those types in Data safety (approximate location, device or other IDs, app activity, diagnostics) and that data is shared with third parties for advertising / analytics as Google describes.
  • Data in transit for ads is handled by Google’s SDK.
  • Users can delete local game data by clearing app storage or uninstalling.
  • No player account.
  • Ads are declared; the app is not “ad-free”.
