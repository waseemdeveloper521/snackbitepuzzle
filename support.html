Common questions

The game lost my levels

Progress is stored only on this device. Uninstalling, clearing storage, or switching phones deletes it. We cannot restore a local save from our side because we never receive it.

There is no sound

Open Settings and check Sound and Music. Also check the phone’s silent switch and volume. The app may use the audio-settings permission only to play effects, not to record you.

How do I change light or dark theme?

On the home screen, open Settings and use the night / theme control. In a level, use the sun/moon button in the corner.

How do hints work?

Hint is optional. When you tap Hint, a rewarded video ad may play. If you watch it through, the game moves one snake one step toward a valid solution. If you close the ad early, you do not get a hint. You can always keep playing without hints.

When do other ads appear?

Full-screen interstitial ads may appear at natural breaks, such as after you finish a level and tap Next level. They are limited so they do not show on every single win. Ads need an internet connection. Puzzles themselves still work offline.

Ads, purchases, or a subscription?

The game is free and supported by Google AdMob (interstitial + rewarded). There are no in-app purchases or subscriptions in this version. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I delete my data?

See Delete your data. There is no cloud account to close.
